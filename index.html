Clear Answers to Your Questions

Everything you need to know about investing with Buffy.com.

Getting started takes less than 3 minutes. Click "Sign Up", complete your identity verification, select your preferred investment plan (starting from $100), and link your bank account or fund via wire/crypto transfer.

All financial market investments carry risk of capital loss. Asset prices fluctuate based on macroeconomic conditions, corporate earnings, interest rates, and market sentiment. Buffy.com utilizes automated diversification and risk management algorithms to help mitigate down-market exposure, but past performance is not a guarantee of future returns.

We deploy bank-grade 256-bit AES encryption for all data in transit and at rest. Client securities are held with regulated custodian institutions and protected by SIPC coverage up to $500,000 (including $250,000 for cash claims). Cash reserves are maintained in FDIC-insured partner bank accounts.

Buffy.com offers access to global Equities, Exchange-Traded Funds (ETFs), Government & Corporate Bonds, Real Estate Trusts (REITs), Precious Metals & Commodities, and high-cap Digital Assets.

Deposits can be made via ACH, Wire Transfer, SEPA, Debit/Credit Card, or approved cryptocurrency wallets. Withdrawals can be requested anytime directly from your User Dashboard and are processed within 24 business hours with zero penalty fees.

Deposit Guidelines & Compliance Reminders

  • Name Matching: Always use payment methods registered in your own legal name. Third-party deposits are strictly prohibited under AML regulations.
  • Verification: Verify account numbers, routing details, or wallet addresses before confirming your transaction.
  • Identity Check: Complete KYC identity verification if requested to enable higher deposit thresholds.
  • Processing Times: Processing speeds vary by gateway (instant for cards/crypto, 1-2 days for bank wires).
